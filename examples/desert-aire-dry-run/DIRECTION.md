# Creative Direction — Desert Aire Heating & Cooling

## Direction: Desert Editorial Warmth

Warm paper canvas, espresso ink, one heat accent. Editorial serif display
(Fraunces) over a plainspoken workhorse body (Nunito Sans), with DM Mono
uppercase eyebrows carrying the trust chassis ("SAME-DAY · MESA, AZ ·
ROC #329417"). Atmosphere comes from type, paper-grain texture, and
heat-horizon gradient accents — no photography (brief supplies none, bans
stock people; typographic specificity carries the trust load instead).

**Traces:** brief style line ("light luxury with desert warmth… NOT the
blue-gradient contractor template") · trends-delta Direction A (pack trend 3
"Nature Distilled" names premium trades; in-market proofs: Altruistic AC's
typography-led register, Goettl's desert-premium in this exact geography) ·
competitors.md gap 1 (nobody pairs same-day promise with repair-first
honesty; nobody shows a license on-page) · personas.md objection→proof map.

## Conversion armature (dual journey — trends-delta niche delta)

- **Emergency path** ("No-Cool Right Now", mobile, 115°): phone number as
  text in header + hero, sticky mobile call bar every page, same-day badge
  above the fold. Luxury styling never costs the call button.
- **Planned path** ("Repair or Replace?", property managers): short quote
  form (name, phone, service; note optional), repair-first pledge copy,
  FAQ handling cost questions without prices (brief rule).

## Messaging spine (copywriter's north star)

"We'll shoot you straight." Same-day speed + repair-first honesty + visible
license. Hero owns the uncontested position from competitors.md: quote
today, fixed tomorrow, and we'll tell you when a repair is enough. Every
claim traces to the brief; no invented stats, no discount positioning,
no prices.

## Motion language

Calm and warm: reveal-on-scroll with generous ease-out, staggered list
entrances, one hero entrance choreography (eyebrow → H1 → proof line → CTA),
heat-gradient slow drift in the hero background (CSS, subtle). No parallax
circus, no marquees, no spinning badges. Reduced-motion: instant.

## Deliberately NOT doing

Blue/white palette · gradient-blob centered hero · stock people · carousels
· on-load popups/chat · credibility buried in footer · long forms · fake
urgency/countdowns · white-on-persimmon CTA labels (3.0:1 — fails) ·
uniform card-grid sameness · brutalism/neon/dark-mode trend tourism.

## Asset plan

Typographic OG cards (code-generated, 1200×630, tokens + Fraunces).
Favicon: monogram "DA" in persimmon on paper. Zero AI imagery (consent: no).
